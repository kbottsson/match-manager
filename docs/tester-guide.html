Match Manager

A small app for running a 7-a-side match: playing time, substitutions, goals and assists. Made for a phone on the touchline — big buttons, works with no signal.

kbottsson.com

No account, no cost, no app store. It's an early build, so expect rough edges — that's what I want your eyes on.

1Put it on your home screen

Open the link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Then it opens full-screen, works offline, and keeps its data — Safari clears stored data for sites you don't visit for a week.

2Add your players — once

Roster tab → first name and shirt number for each kid. First names only. Everything stays on your phone: nothing is uploaded and I can't see it.

3Make a team

Teams tab → + New team → name it (e.g. "P16 A") → tap the players you want → Save. Roster is "everyone in the club"; a team is "the group that plays together". You can have several, and edit them any time.

4Plan a match

Match tab → + Plan another match → opponent, date, which team. Then fill the grid: tap a cell and pick a player. A period is three blocks of 7 / 7 / 6 minutes, so two substitutions per period. The bench row under each period shows who sits out; the forecast at the bottom shows everyone's minutes. Can't make it? Untick them under "Who is here today" — their cells empty and they drop off the bench.

5On match day

  • Start period 1 on the whistle; Stop / Start if play stops.
  • ⚽ on a row = goal, then pick the assist (or No assist).
  • ⇄ on a row = substitution: from a player on the pitch it asks who comes on; from the bench it asks who comes off. Two taps either way.
  • Tap a player's name to change position, sub off, or edit minutes.
  • The period chip at the top holds End period and End match; the ‹ top-left goes home mid-match, clock still running.
Good to know

Minutes include time in goal — one number per player.

Every action can be undone: a toast appears for a few seconds, and there's a ⟲ in the bottom bar.

Nothing syncs yet — what you record stays on your phone.

Known gaps

One coach only; no shared read-only view for a second phone.

No hand-editing of minutes, no season totals.

No availability calendar — you mark absences per match.
